Ken Kahn's Ani (MIT, 1978), running again

Ani's Cinderella

Ani, the program in Ken Kahn's 1979 thesis, turned a short description of Cinderella into this film. Play it, then click a character to see what Ani decided it should do, and why.

The film is drawn from a record of every frame Director filmed when this revival ran Ani's scene programs, four frames a second on a 550 by 413 picture, as Ken's camera took them. The reasons are what Ani herself recorded while planning; the plain-English readings (dotted underline) are ours, and each one opens to Ani's own words.

Pass 1, where Ani chose each character's way of moving, has its own page: Ani's Choice Points.
